# Evidence-driven writing and editable figures

Use the three existing writing/figure skills as primary specialists. These node
contracts augment them; they do not replace the full paper workflow with templates.

## W01 Venue template and budget

Retrieve current official venue/year/track/stage template and rules. Reserve space
for central evidence and actual novelty/limits, not a universal section length.
Record page and appendix budget, anonymity, bibliography and accessibility rules.

## W02 Thesis and narrative

Write problem/consequence → observed gap → falsifiable insight → intervention →
evidence → boundary. Choose framing after evidence audit, with uncertainty and
strongest simple competitor. An unexpected result can change central claim and
motivation; record why rather than rewrite history as a prespecified discovery.

## W03 Main and appendix

Main must contain evidence needed to understand/evaluate central claims, serious
adverse findings and meaningful limits. Appendix supplies full proofs, detailed
configs, expanded tables, prompts and reproducibility. Do not hide a result that
changes the reader's interpretation. Link every appendix item from its obligation.

## W04 Section blueprint

Each section has question, allowed conclusion, supporting sources/data, paragraph
duties and transition. Structure by reader's reasoning, not work chronology.
Maintain claims, symbols, figures and main/appendix allocation across the paper.

## W05 Paragraph and citation

Plan paragraph purpose, topic statement, verified evidence, interpretation and
connection; adapt order for scientific clarity. Separate report from inference.
Use citation at the claim it supports, not decorative citation clusters. Retrieve
official proceedings/publisher BibTeX for verified final versions under L03;
verify author/title/year/venue/DOI and cited proposition. arXiv-only sources retain
their actual status/version. Missing metadata stays unresolved. No automatic
formal-version substitution when the cited content differs.

## W06 Introduction and related work

Motivation follows verified natural failure and remaining gap. Organize related
work by functional differences and strongest alternatives, including unfavorable
comparisons. Distinguish concurrent work from antecedents and unchecked reports.
If simple methods suffice, remove claims that complexity is necessary.

## W07 Method, results and discussion

Describe actual train/inference algorithm, information, configs and derivations.
Results disclose task, denominator, metric, budget and uncertainty. Discussion
separates observed improvements from mechanism explanation and generalization.
Claims match V closure; planned experiments are plans, not results.

## W08 Title, abstract and conclusion

Use one stable supported takeaway. Match numbers/units/scope to tables and method.
Avoid superlatives, causal/general claims beyond evidence and invented acceptance.
Distinguish project's practical release from proof of an autonomous scientific lab.

## W09 Result-driven propagation

On changed results/claims/literature, list dependent motivation, introduction,
method/results/discussion, title/abstract, tables/figures/captions, appendix, website
and posts. Revise affected content and citation versions, preserve draft lineage
and recheck whole-paper consistency before submission. Do not rerun unrelated
experiments because prose changed. Partial/stale drafts remain clearly labeled.

## F01 Method and motivation vectors

Prefer editable SVG plus draw.io/source when requested for exact mechanism graphs.
Use selectable live text, semantic groups/layers, consistent IDs, meaningful
arrows/labels and a legend. Keep equations/data faithful to source. Deliver source,
fonts/style assumptions and final-render view; allow human changes to text/layout.
Raster conceptual art may be separate, never substituted for exact topology.

## F02 Experimental plots and native tables

Use plotting code with raw/versioned data and export SVG/PDF; keep native editable
table data separately. Preserve units, scale, aggregation, independent unit,
uncertainty and all relevant results. Use original programmatic data after manual
style edits; no hand-drawn scientific values. Interactive views must retain static
export and the same numbers. Avoid converting all text to paths by default.

## F03 Captions and readability

Self-contained captions identify task/settings, meaning of arrows/colors, units,
metric direction, n/independent unit and uncertainty calculation. Cite original
source/version when material. Inspect at final paper size and check color/contrast,
font substitution, clipping, missing symbols and accessible alt text. Human edits
that change semantics require evidence recheck; layout-only edits require render QA.

## F04 Manuscript acceptance

Compile actual final main/appendix template, inspect rendered pages, check page
limits, citations, numerical/symbol consistency, figure/table references and
readability. Retain editable figures, plot source, table data and evidence snapshot.
An attractive SVG or compiled PDF is not a scientific validation result.
