# Human question bank

52 independent questions, activated by the current decision. This is a completeness inventory, not a mandatory interview. Ask at most three material unknowns per turn. Infer from actual assets first, retain sourced answers and uncertainty, respect existing authorization, and keep useful independent work moving. Record question ID, node, trigger, answer/source, decision changed and revisit trigger.
