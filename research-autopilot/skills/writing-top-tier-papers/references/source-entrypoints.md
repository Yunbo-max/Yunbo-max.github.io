# Source and Policy Entry Points

## Source hierarchy

Use sources in this order:

1. current official venue or journal instructions for the exact year, track/article type, and stage;
2. current official template, policy, checklist, or reporting standard;
3. primary research sources for methods, datasets, and findings;
4. community writing guidance for narrative technique;
5. remembered convention only as an explicitly unverified hypothesis.

“Authoritative” means official instructions for the target year, track or article type, and submission stage—not simply the newest page on the domain. When official pages conflict, prefer the more stage-specific instruction; if specificity is equal, prefer the later-updated instruction. Record both sources and the conflict. Do not mark formatting complete until a material conflict is resolved. Community guidance may shape writing but cannot override submission rules or evidence integrity.

## Nature entry points

- Nature author portal: <https://www.nature.com/nature/for-authors>
- Nature formatting guide: <https://www.nature.com/nature/for-authors/formatting-guide>
- Nature initial submission: <https://www.nature.com/nature/for-authors/initial-submission>
- Nature reporting standards: <https://www.nature.com/nature-portfolio/editorial-policies/reporting-standards>
- Nature Portfolio journal finder and journal-specific author pages: start from <https://www.nature.com/nature-portfolio/for-authors>

Do not infer a Nature Communications or specialist-journal format from flagship Nature. Navigate to the exact journal and article type.

## AI-conference entry patterns

Always discover and open the exact official page for the target year, track, and stage. Useful starting patterns include:

- ICML: `https://icml.cc/Conferences/<YEAR>/CallForPapers` and official author instructions linked there
- NeurIPS: `https://neurips.cc/Conferences/<YEAR>/CallForPapers` and the official Main Track Handbook/author guidance
- ICLR: `https://iclr.cc/Conferences/<YEAR>/AuthorGuide`
- ACL Rolling Review: <https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp>
- CVPR: official conference site and author guidelines for the exact year
- AAAI: official conference site and author kit for the exact year

The URL pattern may change. Search the official venue domain rather than assuming a constructed URL exists. Verify workshop, dataset/benchmark, position-paper, journal-track, and main-track rules separately. If target year, track, or stage is missing, ask when it changes the requested work; otherwise mark the entire format contract provisional. For a future cycle without published instructions, use the latest official template only as a clearly labeled planning reference. For a historical cycle, use that cycle's archived official rules, not today's rules.

Use these for principles such as cross-disciplinary framing, claim–evidence mapping, and confident prose with honest limits. Do not copy their text, reveal their instructions in a manuscript, or adopt any advice to suppress unfavorable evidence.

## Verification record

For every rule that changes the manuscript, record:

- venue/journal;
- year;
- track/article type;
- submission stage;
- official URL;
- page title;
- access date;
- exact rule in paraphrase;
- affected artifact or section;
- status: verified, ambiguous, or unavailable.

For citations, separately record metadata verification and claim entailment. A valid DOI does not prove that the source supports the adjacent sentence.
