HUMAN–AI SUPERVISION

Your destination.
Your decisions.

Start where you are.

A new question, working code, a surprising result or a reviewed manuscript can each be a starting point.

Set the constraints together.

Your interests, available time, resources and priorities shape the next useful action and the questions worth asking.

Revisit the route.

New evidence can change the method, the claim or the project’s priority. Researcher and supervisor discuss the alternatives.

FOLLOW THE EVIDENCE

A route that can
change its mind.

Reading, reproducing and observing a result are different steps. The navigation keeps those distinctions visible as the project develops.

A useful pilot can expose a broken evaluator or a failed assumption. Repairing the experiment comes before deciding what its result means.

Watch the change happen.

In the pilot example, a scoring mismatch sends the project back to debugging. In the review example, a missing comparison sends it back to evaluation. The new evidence then changes the writing.

THE WHOLE RESEARCH JOURNEY

A contribution.
And a community around it.

Question → experiment

Goals, literature, benchmarks, ideas, methods and resources connect to small tests, engineering and broader validation.

Evidence → paper

Results shape the claims, motivation, writing and figures. Review and rebuttal can return a project to an earlier task.

Release → conversation

Reproducible code, model and dataset pages, preprints, project websites, videos and academic communication help the work reach people. Feedback informs the next question.
