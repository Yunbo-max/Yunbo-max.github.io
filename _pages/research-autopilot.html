          INSIDE THE MAP

          16 modules.
          From question to contribution.

          Open any module to see the research tasks it contains. Literature, implementation, benchmarks and experiment design stay connected as results arrive.

          P

          Projects & resources

          5 tasks+

          Set research goals, inventory project assets, choose priorities, and plan time, GPU capacity and collaboration.

          1. P01Research goals & portfolio
          2. P02Project assets & position
          3. P03Priorities & active projects
          4. P04Time, compute & collaborators
          5. P05Learning & retrospectives
          I

          Research entry & direction

          4 tasks+

          Start from a question, working method, results or reviews. Identify the current decision, contribution type, venue and stopping criteria.

          1. I01Entry point & current decision
          2. I02Contribution type
          3. I03Venue & submission stage
          4. I04Finish, pause or stop criteria
          L

          Literature & implementation

          5 tasks+

          Keep reading papers alongside actual code, supplementary material and benchmark protocols. Update the literature map when new results change the question.

          1. L01Problem & mechanism search
          2. L02Topic map & citation expansion
          3. L03Critical paper reading
          4. L04Code, supplements & reproduction
          5. L05Coverage & new evidence
          B

          Tasks, benchmarks & failures

          5 tasks+

          Define the task and native scoring, qualify strong simple baselines, and establish their genuine remaining failures before proposing a method.

          1. B01Task & metric contract
          2. B02Natural failure census
          3. B03Strong simple baseline qualification
          4. B04Freeze the parent problem
          5. B05Natural Gate 0
          H

          Ideas & critical review

          5 tasks+

          Turn remaining failures into competing explanations, falsifiable claims and decisive predictions. Check necessity, novelty and nearby work.

          1. H01Failure explanations & assumptions
          2. H02Necessity & strongest objections
          3. H03Atomic claims & decisive predictions
          4. H04Functional collision review
          5. H05Importance-preserving candidate selection
          M

          Methods & mathematics

          5 tasks+

          Connect an intervention to its expected mechanism. Make the mathematics computable, define the algorithm, and examine complexity and assumptions.

          1. M01Causal chain & intervention
          2. M02Mathematics & computability
          3. M03Minimal algorithm & interfaces
          4. M04Complexity & scope
          5. M05Components & theoretical obligations
          S

          Models, data & setup

          5 tasks+

          Choose existing benchmarks, inspect data and splits, select models, and freeze fair comparisons and complete run configurations.

          1. S01Benchmark selection matrix
          2. S02Data & split audit
          3. S03Model & API selection
          4. S04Baselines & fair comparison
          5. S05Complete run configuration
          G

          Decisive experiment design

          5 tasks+

          Design the experiment that can distinguish the candidate explanation from its alternatives. Freeze controls, scoring, precision, budget and decision rules.

          1. G01Decisive experiment design
          2. G02Frozen protocol & decision rules
          3. G03Implementation & baseline checks
          4. G04Prompt, inference or training execution
          5. G05Gate A assessment & route choice
          E

          Engineering & debugging

          5 tasks+

          Fix environments and dependencies, check implementation semantics, manage execution, and diagnose bugs or parameter sensitivity while retaining failed runs.

          1. E01Environment & dependencies
          2. E02Semantics, unit checks & canaries
          3. E03Task queue & resource monitoring
          4. E04Root-cause diagnosis & protocol branches
          5. E05Raw evidence & recoverable checkpoints
          V

          Validation & boundaries

          6 tasks+

          Connect every claim to evidence: repeated comparisons, mechanism checks, ablations, generalization, cost, failures and statistical uncertainty.

          1. V01Claims-to-experiments matrix
          2. V02Strong baselines & repeated validation
          3. V03Mechanisms, ablations & replacements
          4. V04Generalization, robustness & scope
          5. V05Quality, cost & failures
          6. V06Statistical checks & evidence freeze
          W

          Writing & result-driven revision

          9 tasks+

          Build the paper around supported claims. Develop the narrative, sections and sentences, and propagate new findings through the manuscript.

          1. W01Venue template & length
          2. W02Central claim & narrative
          3. W03Main paper & appendix
          4. W04Section blueprint
          5. W05Paragraphs & sentences
          6. W06Introduction, motivation & related work
          7. W07Method, results & discussion
          8. W08Title, abstract & conclusion
          9. W09Propagating new results
          F

          Figures & manuscript checks

          4 tasks+

          Create method figures and editable result plots from identified evidence. Check captions, references, readability and consistency in the compiled manuscript.

          1. F01Method & motivation figures
          2. F02Result plots & editable tables
          3. F03Captions, references & readability
          4. F04Compilation & manuscript consistency
          R

          Submission, review & rebuttal

          5 tasks+

          Prepare the submission, track reviewer questions, prioritize justified follow-up experiments, write responses and manage final decisions or resubmission.

          1. R01Submission lock & anonymous materials
          2. R02Reviewer issue ledger
          3. R03Response strategy & follow-up priorities
          4. R04Point-by-point response & revision
          5. R05Decision, resubmission & final manuscript
          A

          Reproducibility & release

          5 tasks+

          Prepare reproducible GitHub releases, Hugging Face assets and demos, arXiv versions and a shared release manifest. Follow reproduction feedback.

          1. A01GitHub reproduction & versions
          2. A02Hugging Face models, data & demos
          3. A03arXiv versions & metadata
          4. A04Shared release manifest
          5. A05User feedback, reproduction & revision
          C

          Websites & communication

          5 tasks+

          Develop project pages, posters, talks, videos, public communication and academic outreach, alongside a personal research homepage and knowledge base.

          1. C01Project page & interactive demo
          2. C02Poster, talk & demonstration video
          3. C03X, LinkedIn & Xiaohongshu
          4. C04Recipient discovery & email drafts
          5. C05Homepage, CV & research knowledge base

          Exploration & map maintenance

          6 tasks+

          Navigate across modules, inspect visual and code evidence, explore counter-explanations within the agreed scope, and maintain the map as capabilities change.

          1. X01Visual position & route exploration
          2. X02PDF figures, diagrams & appendices
          3. X03Code, log & website evidence
          4. X04Bounded exploration & counter-evidence
          5. X05Registering & checking new routes
          6. X06Map quality & capability registry
