    THE IDEA & METHOD TREE

    There is more than
    one way to a new method.

    A method can begin with a real failure, a missing factor, a surprising result or a simpler explanation. Every branch returns to the same questions: why should it work, what else could explain it, and what would falsify it?

    The strong-baseline → real-condition gap → minimal-intervention route comes from your design discussion. These are exploration routes; adding a module does not guarantee improvement or novelty.

    EVIDENCE HAS MORE THAN ONE AXIS

    A paper is a source.
    A result needs conditions.

    What we recordWhat it tells usWhat still needs checking
    arXiv / submitted / accepted / publishedThe publication identity and official version.Whether the particular claim is supported.
    Oral / Spotlight / PosterA verified conference distinction, useful for reading priority.Reproduction, measurement and scope.
    Abstract / scientific read / forensic readWhich parts and versions were actually inspected.Unread appendices, code and assumptions.
    Code runs / result matched / independent replicationWhat was reproduced, by whom and under which settings.Untested claims, conditions and generalization.
    Author report / direct observation / inferenceHow a statement connects to evidence.Its uncertainty and competing explanations.

    A question mark stays visible.

    Unreproduced arXiv empirical results are leads or author reports. They do not become direct experimental evidence for a new project. Accepted papers also need actual verification before being treated as our measurements.

    The citation follows the content.

    Prefer an official final conference or journal citation when the matching version supports the statement. Keep arXiv versions when the relevant content differs. Never invent a venue, DOI or distinction.

    EXPERIMENTS HAVE A DEVELOPMENT LOOP

    Room to debug.
    A clear point to confirm.

    Develop

    Reproduce the baseline, repair code and explore hyperparameters on development data. Keep all attempts and failures.

    Qualify

    Check semantics, scorer, strong baseline and resource feasibility. An engineering fault is inconclusive evidence.

    Freeze & confirm

    Lock the protocol before seeing held-out results. A result-affecting change creates a child protocol.

    Decide & expand

    PASS, REVISE, KILL or INCONCLUSIVE. Expand only to close claim-specific evidence gaps.

    One QA score can hide different tasks.

    Sampling once or many times, majority vote, best-of-k, pass@k, answer normalization, judge prompts and micro/macro averages must be explicit. Report coverage and scoring failures alongside the number.

    Resources belong to the experiment.

    Model, dataset and evaluator choices match the research goal and budget. Separate environments, directories and job identities protect concurrent runs; allocation and GPU memory require actual monitoring.

    Full validation follows the actual claim: strong baselines, repeats and uncertainty, mechanism tests, generalization, costs and failures. NeurIPS reporting guidance is a reference, not an acceptance guarantee.

    THE WORK CONTINUES AFTER THE EXPERIMENT

    Write. Review.
    Release. Reach people.

    W / F · A paper you can check

    Results reshape the thesis and motivation. Main and appendix each have a purpose. Sections, paragraphs, citations, editable SVGs and tables remain tied to evidence.

    R / A · A contribution you can use

    Independent model and human reviews, issue-by-issue rebuttal, reproducible GitHub/HF artifacts and an arXiv-ready handoff. Complex author-portal steps can go to the human.

    C / X · A visible body of work

    Project pages, demos, videos, X, LinkedIn, 小红书 and relevant academic contact. Feedback returns to the evidence map and project record.

    RESEARCH AUTOPILOT · RELEASE 1.1

    Bring a question.
    Start with one skill.

    The release includes Research Autopilot, Writing Top-Tier Papers, Designing Pipeline Figures and Designing Experiment Figures. The 84 node entries load exact sections from this shared skill bundle.

    Get the skills on GitHub ↗Release manifest →
    1. Open the repository’s research-autopilot/skills/ folder and copy the four skill directories to the location supported by your agent.
    2. Keep all four directories together so node-level writing and figure references can resolve.
    3. Invoke research-autopilot with your question, existing assets and the decision you want to make.
    python3 research-autopilot/skills/research-autopilot/scripts/research_nodes.py check
python3 research-autopilot/skills/research-autopilot/scripts/research_nodes.py show L03

    This release provides skills, protocols, source navigation and a public map. Execution adapters, paid resources and account connections depend on your environment. There is no automatic all-project scheduler in this release.
