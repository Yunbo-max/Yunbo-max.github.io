WHY THIS MATTERS NOW

More automated work.
More visible evaluation.

As research agents produce more ideas, experiments and manuscripts, the evaluation process becomes part of the research landscape we need to understand.

OpenJudge focuses on public records: what a reviewer said, what the author answered, which version was available and what the decision actually was.

Read the evidence before the headline.

A scoring contrast does not prove an incorrect review, an incorrect paper or misconduct. Original submission PDFs and independent experiments are not verified for the full collection. Reports disclose actual reading scope and version limits.

The collection includes 113 current cases and a separate historical section. It reflects the selected archive, with unknowns and conflicts preserved.
