WHO WATCHES THE PROCESS?

Open records are a beginning.
Observation is ongoing work.

OpenReview provides a platform for papers, reviews and discussion. Making a record visible opens another question: who keeps examining how arguments, responses and decisions fit together across the system?

We want to build that independent perspective. OpenJudge uses AI-assisted reading to compare claims with their sources, follow changes in a discussion and surface cases worth understanding. The aim is to learn where peer review works, where it struggles, and what might make it more reliable.

Adaptive Stacking · Learning What to Remember for Non-Markovian Reinforcement Learning

The discussion distinguished the capacity of a memory stack from the length of history an environment requires. The area chair identified that confusion in a critique of the theory. The paper was still rejected, with concerns about evaluation scope and comparisons remaining.

THE OBSERVER’S QUESTION

When a central misunderstanding is corrected, how should the remaining objections be weighed?

Follow the change in the discussion

The archived meta-review acknowledges the distinction between a small memory buffer and a much longer relevant history. It also identifies gaps in complex visual evaluation and comparisons with specialized memory architectures.

This lets us separate the correction of a particular argument from an assessment of the whole paper. The published analysis uses the available discussion and manuscript material; no independent experiment reproduction is claimed.

The archive shows ratings of 2, 2, 4 and 2, alongside a poster acceptance. Reviewers questioned novelty and evaluation. The area chair valued the engineering contribution and cited additional comparisons and clarifications in the response.

THE OBSERVER’S QUESTION

How should a useful engineering contribution be judged when reviewers disagree about what counts as novelty?

Look beyond the numerical contrast

The meta-review describes additional budget-matched comparisons and ablations. It also expresses disagreement with some novelty objections and with the expectation that an attack paper must propose defenses.

A prediction that reviewers might raise their ratings is distinct from a recorded rating change. The archived mean is a dated snapshot, and the acceptance needs to be read alongside the arguments and process context.

AI AS AN OBSERVER

Read closely.
Leave room to be corrected.

An observation becomes useful when another reader can trace it, challenge it and offer a better explanation. That is the standard we want OpenJudge to work toward.

  1. 01 / THE RECORD

    Keep the source in view.

    Identify the paper version, review, author response and decision. Preserve the dates and distinguish archived scores from later discussion.

  2. 02 / THE INTERPRETATION

    Compare the arguments.

    Follow an objection through the response and meta-review. Examine reasonable alternative explanations and state what was actually read.

  3. 03 / THE OPEN QUESTION

    Make revision possible.

    Show what remains uncertain and which evidence could change the interpretation. Invite corrections from authors, reviewers and readers.
