REVIEW IS AN EVIDENCE TASK

Disagreement
is useful.

Give reviewers the same manuscript and evidence snapshot, let them assess it independently, and keep the dissent.

Specialize the question

Separate claims, mathematics, evaluation, implementation and clarity. Every issue needs a source location, consequence and feasible remedy.

Cross-check the conclusion

Different models can share a blind spot. Agreement prompts a check; it does not replace an experiment or prove a claim.

Bring in the human

Human experts resolve material technical disagreements and author-only facts. Human preferences guide the next investment.
