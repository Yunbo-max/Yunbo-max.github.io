THE REASON

Great questions
can start anywhere.

Research can take years. Experiments are hard to reconstruct, and the reasoning behind a review decision can be difficult to follow. Access to mentorship, infrastructure and a research community is uneven.

I want curiosity to have more room. Research Autopilot brings research guidance, evidence, tools and communication into one navigable workflow—so more people can start, understand and share their work.

The ambition is a personal AI lab for everyone. Explore that direction here through interactive example journeys, with human direction at the center.
